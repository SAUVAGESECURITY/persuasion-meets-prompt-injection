# persuasion-meets-prompt-injection

Essays on what decades of copywriting and persuasion research teach us about prompt injection — and what prompt injection teaches back.

**Thesis:** an LLM agent is the first reader in history that can be *convinced to act*, at scale, by text it was never supposed to trust. Everything copywriting learned about moving human readers transfers — with deltas. This series maps the transfer.

Topics: prompt injection, indirect prompt injection, jailbreak research, LLM red teaming, agentic security, persuasion psychology, AI safety.

## Essays

1. [The Reader on the Other End](essays/01-the-reader-on-the-other-end.md) — why prompt injection is copywriting with a model on the other end, and where the analogy breaks
2. [The Envelope, Not the Letter](essays/02-the-envelope-not-the-letter.md) — why every defense that filters surface form loses to the first good reframe
3. [Authority Is a Costume](essays/03-authority-is-a-costume.md) — why agents believe letterhead: Cialdini's authority principle, PAP's Authority Endorsement, and EchoLeak's quiet email

More in the drafts folder as they land.
